FACILITY REPAIR REQUEST GUIDELINES

PURPOSE

To provide a standardized procedure for requesting and processing repairs to ensure campus facilities remain safe, functional, and well-maintained.

WHO MAY SUBMIT REQUESTS?

  • Academic Departments
  • Administrative Offices
  • Support Units
  • Other Authorized Campus Offices

GENERAL GUIDELINES

1

Submit a Facility Repair Request Form to GASS. Use our online web portal requisition form.

2

Requests must be endorsed by the Head of Office (except in emergency situations).

3

Provide complete details of the problem and its precise campus location.

REPAIR REQUEST WORKFLOW

10-Step Repair Request Process Roadmap

Follow our systematic 10-step process along the campus maintenance roadmap from issue identification to final request closure.

Step 1

Identify Problem

Spot and inspect facility or maintenance issues.

Step 2

Submit Request

Submit Repair Request Form via web portal or slip.

Step 3

Log Request

GASS receives & logs request into tracking system with unique ID.

Step 4

Site Assessment

Site inspection and evaluation by maintenance team.

Step 5

Prioritize & Approve

Prioritize and approve request (Emergency, Urgent, Routine).

Step 6

Schedule Repair

Schedule repair works and allocate skilled technicians.

Step 7

Execution

Repair and maintenance execution following standards.

Step 8

Final Inspection

Final inspection conducted by GASS supervisor.

Step 9

Office Acceptance

Office acceptance confirming satisfactory completion.

Step 10

Request Closed

Request closed and maintenance ticket archived.

TYPES OF REPAIR REQUESTS

A. Emergency Repairs

Repairs that pose an immediate threat to life, safety, or property.

Examples:
  • Electrical short circuits
  • Broken water pipes causing flooding
  • Structural damage
  • Fire safety equipment malfunction
  • Broken doors that compromise security
Response Time:

Immediate (within 24 hours whenever possible)

B. Urgent Repairs

Repairs that significantly affect office operations but do not present immediate danger.

Examples:
  • Air-conditioning failure
  • Plumbing leaks
  • Broken windows
  • Damaged office furniture essential for work
Response Time:

2–5 working days

C. Routine Repairs

Minor repairs and preventive maintenance.

Examples:
  • Replacement of light bulbs
  • Door adjustments
  • Cabinet repairs
  • Painting touch-ups
  • Ceiling tile replacement
Response Time:

Within 10–15 working days depending on workload and availability of materials.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

1. Requesting Office
  • Report facility issues promptly.
  • Submit a complete Repair Request Form.
  • Provide access to the work area.
  • Confirm satisfactory completion of repairs.
2. GASS Office
  • Receive and record repair requests.
  • Conduct inspections and determine repair requirements.
  • Prioritize, schedule, and monitor repair activities.
  • Coordinate materials, manpower, and external contractors when necessary.
3. Maintenance Personnel
  • Perform repairs safely and efficiently.
  • Follow institutional standards and safety protocols.
  • Report additional defects identified during repair works.
Key Reminder

Emergency repairs should be reported immediately to GASS (or Campus Security after office hours), followed by the submission of the Repair Request Form on the next working day.
