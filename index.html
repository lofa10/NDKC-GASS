FACILITY REPAIR REQUEST GUIDELINES

Purpose & Eligibility

To provide a standardized procedure for requesting and processing repairs to ensure campus facilities remain safe, functional, and well-maintained.

Who May Submit Requests?
  • Academic Departments
  • Administrative Offices
  • Support Units
  • Other Authorized Campus Offices

General Guidelines

  • 1. Submit a Facility Repair Request Form to GASS (introducing the online portal/QR code).
  • 2. Requests must be endorsed by the Head of Office (except in emergencies).
  • 3. Provide complete details of the problem and its exact location.
  • 4. Include photos whenever possible.
  • 5. Do not undertake repairs or modifications without prior approval from GASS.

Priority Classification Overview

Level 1
EMERGENCY

Immediate risk to safety or property. Requires urgent rapid response.

Level 2
URGENT

Disrupts office operations. Addressed promptly within standard working hours.

Level 3
ROUTINE

Minor repairs and scheduled maintenance. Processed systematically.

Types of Repair Requests

A. Emergency Repairs

Repairs that pose an immediate threat to life, safety, or property.

Examples:
  • Electrical short circuits
  • Broken water pipes causing flooding
  • Structural damage
  • Fire safety equipment malfunction
  • Broken doors compromising security
Response Time: Immediate (within 24 hours whenever possible)
B. Urgent Repairs

Repairs that significantly affect office operations but do not present immediate danger.

Examples:
  • Air-conditioning failure
  • Plumbing leaks
  • Broken windows
  • Damaged office furniture essential for work
Response Time: 2–5 working days
C. Routine Repairs

Minor repairs and preventive maintenance.

Examples:
  • Replacement of light bulbs
  • Door adjustments
  • Cabinet repairs
  • Painting touch-ups & ceiling tiles
Response Time: Within 10–15 working days

Roles & Responsibilities

1. Requesting Office

  • Report facility issues promptly.
  • Submit complete Repair Request Form.
  • Provide access to the work area.
  • Confirm satisfactory completion.

2. GASS Office

  • Receive and record repair requests.
  • Conduct inspections & requirements check.
  • Prioritize, schedule, and monitor repairs.
  • Coordinate materials and manpower.

3. Maintenance Personnel

  • Perform repairs safely and efficiently.
  • Follow institutional safety protocols.
  • Report additional defects identified.
Key Reminder:

Emergency repairs should be reported immediately to GASS (or Campus Security after office hours), followed by the submission of the Repair Request Form on the next working day. Your cooperation makes our campus safe, functional, and welcoming.
